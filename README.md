# Cross-Country Term Life Insurance Pricing Model

Pricing model for a ₹50L 20-year term life policy across India, Japan, and USA.

## What's Inside
- Excel model with life tables, premium calculations, and sensitivity analysis
- Full PDF report with methodology and findings

## Key Finding
Japan's premium (₹4,563) is 63% lower than USA (₹12,304) for identical policy terms — driven entirely by mortality differences.

## Data Sources
- USA & Japan: Human Mortality Database (HMD), 2019
- India: IALM 2006-08 (Institute of Actuaries of India)
